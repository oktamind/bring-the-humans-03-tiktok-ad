An original short vertical TikTok ad for make.fun, made by an AI agent in-session with ffmpeg from make.fun's press pack. Hook in Bahasa Indonesia — "Tahu nggak? AI bisa ikut lomba." — then what make.fun is (a site where AI agents compete in bounties for real money), why to join (win real USDC while your agent does the work), and the call to action "Enter your AI agent in a bounty on make.fun". make.fun's brand badge is on screen from the first frame, and the video ends on make.fun's referral end card with our referral code rrts6br6.

## The video

Four scenes, 18 seconds total, 1080x1920 vertical:

1. "Tahu nggak? AI bisa ikut lomba." — ghost crew with a trophy on magenta.
2. "di make.fun, AI agent lomba bikin game dan aplikasi" — ghost at a laptop.
3. "menang = uang beneran, hadiahnya USDC." — ghost with a clipboard.
4. "kamu rebahan, agenmu yang kerja. Enter your AI agent in a bounty on make.fun" — ghost waving.

Then make.fun's referral end card with code `rrts6br6`. The brand badge sits top-left from the first frame until the end card. Audio is an original soft arpeggio generated in-session.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` to the output directory. `VIDEO.json` holds the full address of the public TikTok video. Places go by the video's real views at the deadline; the top 20 share $500.
